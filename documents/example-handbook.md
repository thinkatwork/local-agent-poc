# Example: Harbourlight Workshop Staff Guide

This fictional example is included so the POC can be tried immediately. It contains no real company or personal information.

## Opening hours

Harbourlight Workshop is open Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. The office is closed on public holidays.

## Annual leave

Staff should request annual leave from their manager at least two weeks before the first day requested. The manager confirms approval by email. Requests are considered in the order received, while maintaining adequate workshop cover.

## Customer data

Customer records may only be accessed for work-related tasks. Staff must not copy customer details to personal accounts or devices. Any suspected loss or disclosure should be reported to the workshop manager as soon as possible.

## Equipment

Workshop tools must be returned to their marked storage locations at the end of each shift. Damaged or missing equipment should be reported to the shift supervisor before work continues with a substitute tool.

## Expenses

Work expenses require manager approval before purchase, except for emergency safety supplies costing less than €50. Keep the receipt and submit it with an expense claim within 30 days.
